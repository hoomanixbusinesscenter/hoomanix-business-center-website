How to Choose the Right Office Size for Your Growing Team

May 30, 2026 4 min read Hoomanix Team

One of the most common mistakes growing companies make in Dubai is either overcommitting to a large private office they don't need yet, or squeezing a growing team into a space that limits collaboration. Here's a practical way to think about sizing your workspace correctly.

Start With Your Headcount Today, Not in a Year

It's tempting to lease for the team you'll have in twelve months, but that means paying for empty desks now. Flexible workspace providers solve this differently — you can size for your current headcount and expand into a larger suite as you actually hire, without renegotiating a long-term lease.

The Rule of Thumb: Square Feet Per Person

As a general guide, allow roughly 70–100 square feet per person for a comfortable private office setup, including desk space, walkways, and shared areas like a small meeting corner. Open-plan coworking setups can run more efficiently, closer to 50–70 square feet per person.

When to Choose a Hot Desk vs. a Private Office vs. an Enterprise Floor

The right format depends on team size and how much privacy your work requires:

  • Hot Desk — ideal for solo freelancers and remote workers who need a professional space a few days a week, without a long-term commitment.
  • Private Office — best for teams of 2–15 who need a dedicated, lockable space with room to grow.
  • Enterprise Floor — suited to larger teams of 15+ that need a fully custom layout and dedicated infrastructure.
"Most teams don't actually need more desks — they need better access to meeting rooms when they do need to collaborate."

Plan for Meeting Room Access, Not Just Desks

A workspace that's slightly smaller on desk count but includes easy, affordable access to meeting rooms often works better than an oversized office that sits half-empty most days. Factor in how often your team actually needs a room, not just how many people you employ.

Signs You've Outgrown Your Current Space

Recurring signs include team members regularly working from cafes because the office is full, meeting rooms booked out days in advance, or storage and equipment spilling into walkways. Any of these is a signal to reassess your plan before it starts affecting productivity.

The Bottom Line

The right office size is less about predicting the future and more about choosing a provider that lets you adjust as you grow. Hoomanix offers Hot Desk, Private Office, and Enterprise Floor plans on the same Sheikh Zayed Road address, so scaling up never means switching buildings.
