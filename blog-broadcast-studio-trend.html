Why More Dubai Brands Are Recording Content In-House at Broadcast Studios

June 8, 2026 4 min read Hoomanix Team

More Dubai brands, agencies, and solo creators are moving podcast recording, product videos, and social content production in-house rather than outsourcing to external production houses. The shift is driven by cost, speed, and creative control — but it only works if the space itself is built for it.

Why In-House Recording Is Gaining Ground

Booking an external production house for every video or podcast episode adds up quickly in both cost and scheduling delays. Teams that record regularly — weekly podcasts, product demos, or social content calendars — save significant time by having studio access on demand rather than booking weeks in advance.

What to Look for in a Rentable Studio

Not every "studio" delivers broadcast-quality results. Before booking, check that the space includes:

  • Proper acoustic soundproofing to eliminate outside noise
  • Professional lighting rigs, not just overhead office lighting
  • HD cameras and a green screen for flexible backgrounds
  • High-speed internet (1 Gbps or better) for live streaming
  • Quality microphones suited to podcast or voiceover recording

Podcasts vs. Video Content: Different Studio Needs

A two-person podcast setup mainly needs strong acoustics and reliable microphones, while product videos or livestreams demand more from lighting, camera angles, and a clean visual backdrop. A good studio should be flexible enough to reconfigure for either.

"The gap between a home recording setup and a proper studio shows up the moment your audience hits play — audio and lighting quality is the first thing people notice."

Cost Comparison: Studio Rental vs. Building Your Own

Outfitting a private studio with soundproofing, lighting, and camera equipment easily runs into tens of thousands of dirhams before you record a single episode. Renting an equipped studio by the hour or day lets brands test content formats and build an audience before committing to that capital expense.

The Bottom Line

As content production moves in-house across Dubai, access to a properly equipped studio — without the upfront investment — is becoming a competitive advantage.
