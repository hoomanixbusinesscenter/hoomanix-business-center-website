Ejari & Trade License: What Every New Business in Dubai Must Know

June 20, 2026 6 min read Hoomanix Team

Every company operating in Dubai needs two things before it can legally open its doors: a registered Ejari contract and a valid trade license. For first-time entrepreneurs, the paperwork can feel confusing — here is exactly what each step involves and how a serviced or virtual office simplifies the process.

What Is Ejari and Why Do You Need It?

Ejari is Dubai's official tenancy registration system, run by the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA). It legally registers your business address and is a mandatory document the Department of Economic Development (DED) or your free zone authority will ask for before issuing a trade license.

Step 1: Secure a Registered Business Address

You cannot apply for Ejari without a valid tenancy contract at a registered commercial address. This is where a serviced or virtual office becomes useful — instead of signing a multi-year lease, you can register your business at a recognized address like Hoomanix's Sheikh Zayed Road location from day one.

Step 2: Register Your Ejari Contract

Once your tenancy agreement is in place, your provider submits the contract to the Ejari system. At Hoomanix, this documentation is prepared and submitted on your behalf as part of the office or virtual office package, so you don't need to navigate the RERA portal yourself.

Step 3: Apply for Your Trade License

With Ejari confirmed, you can submit your trade license application to the DED (for mainland companies) or the relevant free zone authority. Typical documents required at this stage include:

  • Your Ejari certificate
  • Passport copies of all shareholders
  • Proposed company name and business activity
  • Memorandum of Association (for certain company types)
  • Initial approval certificate from the DED or free zone
"Most delays we see aren't about the business idea — they're about a missing Ejari certificate or an address that isn't properly registered."

How Long Does the Process Take?

With a registered address already in place, Ejari registration typically takes 1–2 business days, and a straightforward trade license approval can follow within a week. Businesses that start the process without a valid address often lose weeks simply searching for compliant office space.

Common Mistakes That Delay Approval

The most frequent hold-ups are submitting a tenancy contract that isn't RERA-registered, mismatched company names between documents, and choosing a business activity that doesn't match the licensing authority's category list. Working with a provider that handles Ejari regularly helps you avoid all three.

The Bottom Line

A registered address is the first domino in Dubai business setup — get it right, and Ejari and your trade license tend to follow smoothly. Hoomanix handles the documentation as part of every serviced and virtual office package, so you can focus on actually running your business.
