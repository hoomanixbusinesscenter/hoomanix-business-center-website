Frequently Asked
Questions

Still have questions? Our team is available 24/7 — reach out via WhatsApp or email and we'll get back to you within minutes.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade from Hot Desk to Private Office or Enterprise Floor at any time. Just reach out to our team and we'll handle the entire transition seamlessly.

Is there a minimum contract?

Hot Desk and Private Office plans are fully month-to-month with no lock-in. Enterprise Floor requires a minimum 6-month commitment for facility allocation and custom setup.

Are there any hidden fees?

None whatsoever. All utilities, WiFi, cleaning, security, and maintenance are included in your plan. Meeting room overages are the only billable extra — and we notify you in advance.

Can I visit before signing?

We actively encourage it. Schedule a free, no-obligation tour and our team will walk you through every available space, answer your questions, and help you find the perfect fit.
